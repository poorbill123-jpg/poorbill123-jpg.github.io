Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Short version: your puff numbers stay on your phone, we keep only what the app needs, and we never sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your answers to the start questions, every puff you tap, your plan, your cravings and SOS use, your moods and your lessons are stored on your device. They are not sent to us.

What goes to our server

The app makes an anonymous account (no email, no password). If you join a crew, the server keeps the name you chose, your crew, and your league points: which days you closed, did the check-in or the lesson, and how many full SOS sessions earned points. Friends in your crew see only your name, your points and the days you closed. Never your puffs, and never when you had a craving or used SOS.

The AI coach

If you chat with the coach, your messages are stored so the chat continues, and are sent to Anthropic to write the reply, together with your own plan numbers (for example your day, limit, puffs today and reason). The coach is an AI, not a doctor.

Payments and reminders

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through RevenueCat. We never see your card details. Reminders are set on your phone and only if you say yes.

Who else is involved

Data on our server is stored with Supabase. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical records such as IP addresses for security. No advertising trackers, no cookies for ads.

Deleting everything

In the app: Me › Privacy and data › Delete my account and data. That removes your account and your data for good.
