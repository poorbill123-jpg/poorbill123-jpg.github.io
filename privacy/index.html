Privacy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Short version: we only keep what we need, and we never sell your data.

What we store

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and the time you joined. This website asks for nothing else.

Why we store it

Only to tell you when the Breezy app is ready and to share news about its launch.

Who else is involved

Your email is stored with Supabase, on servers in the EU. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical records of visits, such as IP addresses, for security.

What we don't do

We never sell your data. This website doesn't use advertising trackers or cookies.

Your choice

You can ask us to remove your email at any time, and we will delete it.

The app

This page covers the website. We will update it to explain what the app stores before the app launches.
