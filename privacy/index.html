Privacy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Short version: we only keep what we need, and we never sell your data.

The website

The website itself asks for nothing. If you joined our early access list before, we keep that email only to tell you about the app.

Who else is involved

Your data is stored with Supabase, on servers in the EU. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical records of visits, such as IP addresses, for security.

What we don't do

We never sell your data. This website doesn't use advertising trackers or cookies.

Your choice

You can ask us to remove your data at any time, and we will delete it.

In the app (on your phone or in your browser)

The app stores what it needs to run your plan: what you vape, your weekly spend and currency, your reasons, your puff logs, cravings and what triggered them, missions, badges, your savings goal, and your streak. Your goal photo stays on your device.

If you record a voice note, it is kept in private storage that only your account can read. If you link a buddy, they can only see your streak and how many cravings you have beaten.

If you chat with the AI coach, your messages are stored so the chat stays, and are sent to Anthropic to create replies, along with your first name (if you gave it), plan week, streak, top trigger and reasons. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google with RevenueCat. We never see your card details.

You can delete your account from Me, then Settings, in the app. That removes your account and this data for good.
