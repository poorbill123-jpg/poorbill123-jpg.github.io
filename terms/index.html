Who it's for

Breezy is for adults aged 18 or older. Please only join if you are 18 or older.

Using it in your browser

You can use Breezy right in your browser, and add it to your home screen. Reminders and buddy notifications only work in the phone app. Features may change as the app grows.

The app and subscriptions

Some parts of the app need a subscription, sold through Apple or Google with a free trial. You can cancel any time in your store settings. Breathing help, the money counter and logging are always free.

Not medical advice

Breezy is a self-help tool and doesn't give medical advice. If you have worries about your health or about quitting, please talk to a doctor.

We may update these terms as the app grows. The date at the top shows the latest version.
